# BansheeRap

**Boat charter & brokerage — Puerto Rico**
**In progress · 2026**

> **Engagement status:** This engagement is at the diagnosis and planning stage. The findings and plan below are real; the results are not in yet, and nothing on this page should be read as a completed outcome.

## The Diagnosis

The business converts well and is leaking anyway.

- **No CRM.** Inquiries are tracked by memory. There is no record of who asked about what, when, or why a deal went cold.
- **No card payment rails.** Payment friction sits directly on top of the moment a customer has already decided to buy.
- **No online conversion asset.** Inbound interest has nowhere to land.
- **Thin brokering margins**, which makes every lost inquiry expensive relative to the work already done to earn it.
- **Management contracts lost to friction.** The highest-value line — boat management — has lost contracts to unclear scope and boundary disputes rather than to price or competition.

The close rate today runs roughly **30-40%** on an average deal of **$850+**. That is a healthy conversion rate on low inquiry volume, which points the problem squarely at intake and capacity rather than at persuasion.

## The Plan

1. **Rebuild boat management as a structured product.** Defined scope, explicit boundaries, written terms. Most of the lost contracts trace to ambiguity, not disagreement.
2. **Add payment rails.** ATH Movil for the local market and Stripe for card, so payment stops being a negotiation.
3. **Deploy a CRM layer.** HubSpot with n8n over inquiry intake, so inquiries are captured and attributed instead of remembered.
4. **Build a conversion asset** for inbound interest.

## Why This Order

Payments and CRM are cheap and fast. The management-product work is slower but addresses the largest single loss. Sequencing the quick structural fixes first means the intake data starts accumulating while the harder product work is still underway — so the eventual before/after has a real baseline instead of a reconstructed one.

## What This Page Will Show Later

Inquiry capture rate, close rate against the current 30-40% baseline, and revenue retained on management contracts.
