# ComMotion

**Adaptive dance nonprofit — CDMX / North Carolina**
**In progress · 2026**

> **Engagement status:** This engagement is at the diagnosis and planning stage. The findings and plan below are real; no results exist yet and none are claimed.

## The Diagnosis

Three problems that compound each other.

- **A broken donation funnel.** The single path that converts intent into money does not work reliably.
- **A fragmented brand.** The organization is spread across multiple domains and social handles, so the audience it has already earned cannot reliably find the thing it is looking for.
- **No CRM.** Mailchimp, Eventbrite, Kajabi, and WordPress each hold a partial contact list, and none of them talk. A person who attended an event, subscribed, and donated exists as three unrelated records.

For a nonprofit this is worse than the equivalent commercial problem. Donor relationships compound over years, and a fragmented contact record means the organization cannot see its own history with the people most likely to give again.

## The Plan

1. **Repair the donation flow first.** It is the only item on this list that is losing money every day it stays broken.
2. **Consolidate the brand** onto a single domain and a coherent set of handles.
3. **Unify contacts on HubSpot Free**, with n8n as the integration layer across Mailchimp, Eventbrite, Kajabi, and WordPress.

Follow-on work adds automated grant prospecting against Grants.gov and North Carolina funders — deliberately sequenced last, because prospecting for money the organization cannot yet receive cleanly or steward properly would be premature.

## Why HubSpot Free

Nonprofit budget is a real constraint, not a nice-to-have. The integration layer does the work here; the CRM only has to be the place records land. Choosing a paid tier before the contact data is unified would be paying for capacity against a problem that is not yet defined.
